Radio practice for student and private pilots

Talk to the tower.
See what it does to you.

A simulated tower talks to you, you read back, and the camera follows your pulse and your breathing while you do. Afterwards you see which messages made them climb.

A mirror for practice, not a measure of health. Readback compares you only with yourself, with a calm minute taken at the start. No medical claim, no normal or abnormal values, no "fit to fly". It is a practice game, not an approved training device: your instructor and your country's official phraseology manual are the reference.

Before the camera turns on

What is measured
Your pulse and your breathing rate, from the video of your face and upper body, by the Presage SmartSpectra SDK. The computation runs on this computer.
What stays here
The camera images are never recorded and never sent. Your voice is transcribed on this computer by the tower's speech model, then dropped. The measurements stay in memory and are saved only if you press Save at the end, on this computer only, with a button to delete them.
What goes to Presage
The SDK contacts Presage's service to check your API key and your plan while it measures. Readback turns the SDK's telemetry off, so no usage summary is sent either. No image and no measured value leaves this computer.
How to stop
"Stop camera" at the top stays visible the whole time. Closing the window stops everything. The camera also turns off at the end of each circuit.
